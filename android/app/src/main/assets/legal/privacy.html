Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Interveen is built to stay on your device. This policy explains what the app can see and what it never collects.

What stays on the phone

Address-bar text is read only to match a local blocklist. Matches, your goal video or image, and statistics are stored in Interveen’s private app storage. They are not uploaded.

What we do not collect

We do not operate an account, analytics, or advertising network. Browsing history, matched URLs, and personal media are not sent to Interveen or to third parties by this app.

Permissions

Accessibility is used only to read browser address bars on this device. Display-over-other-apps shows the reminder you chose. Camera and microphone are used only when you record a reminder. Existing photos and videos are chosen through the system photo picker; the app does not request broad photo or video library access. Notifications keep Guard alive.
